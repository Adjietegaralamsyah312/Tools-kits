Frequently Asked Questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. No login, subscription or paywall — monetization uses optional AdSlot placeholders, never blocking.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. JSON, images and text are processed locally. See Privacy.

Do I need an account?

No.

Which browsers are supported?

Modern Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. SHA-256/512 need a secure context (HTTPS/localhost).

How do I request a new tool?

Use the contact form.
