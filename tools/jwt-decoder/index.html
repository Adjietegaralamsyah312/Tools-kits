JWT Decoder

Decode a JWT to read its header and payload. 100% client-side — nothing is uploaded or stored.

⚠️ Decoding is not verification. Anyone can read a JWT payload. Always verify the signature server-side with the secret or public key.

How to use

  1. Paste a JWT (header.payload.signature).
  2. Click Decode.
  3. Read the header and payload; check the signature note.

FAQ

Is my token uploaded?

No. Decoding runs locally in your browser and the token is never stored or sent anywhere.

Does decoding verify the signature?

No. Decoding only reads the token. Verify signatures server-side with the secret or public key.
