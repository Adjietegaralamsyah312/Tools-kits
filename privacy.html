Privacy Policy

Your tool inputs never leave your device. JSON, Base64, images, text and files are processed with browser APIs (Canvas, Web Crypto). We do not upload, store or log them.

  • No accounts, no tool-input database
  • No invasive tracking; analytics hook (ToolskitsAnalytics) is a no-op stub you can wire to a privacy-friendly provider later
  • Theme preference stored only in your browser's localStorage
  • QR page loads jsDelivr CDN library; other pages have zero third-party requests

Contact form data (when wired to a backend) should be handled per your provider's policy. Do not send secrets via the form.
